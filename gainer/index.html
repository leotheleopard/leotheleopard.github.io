03

Beyond just eating more

01
More, smaller meals
Five or six smaller sittings are a lot easier on a full stomach than three big ones.
02
Drink your calories
Shakes and smoothies go down easier than another plate of food, especially later in the day.
03
Add extras to everything
Butter, oil, cheese, or cream stirred into meals you're already eating adds calories with no extra volume.
04
Eat before bed
A last meal or shake before sleep keeps the surplus going overnight instead of an 8-hour gap.
05
Prioritize sleep
7–9 hours supports a steadier appetite and better recovery from the extra food and any activity.
06
Dial back excess cardio
Less burned off during the day means more of the surplus actually sticks.
07
Keep easy snacks around
Nuts, trail mix, or granola bars within reach make it easy to add calories between meals without thinking about it.
08
Track loosely, not obsessively
Being roughly consistent day to day matters more than hitting an exact number every time.
